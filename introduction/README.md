1. Introduction
   
1.1 Background

The Student Representative Council (Majlis Perwakilan Pelajar, MPP) is elected by students to speak for them on campus. Researchers at UiTM describe this as an organisation-level election, held at university level to appoint new MPP representatives (Abd Halim, Razali and Abd Aziz, 2009). In their study, both the back end and the front end of the election were still handled with conventional methods.

The same study notes that information and communication technology (ICT) can reduce the cost and time of an election, as well as the blank votes that are common with ballot papers. It proposes a web-based electronic voting system for MPP elections, redesigns the process using business process re-engineering, and builds a prototype using a system development life cycle. Security, trust and user acceptance are left for further work.

Reference: Abd Halim, K. N., Razali, R., & Abd Aziz, M. E. S. (2009). Re-engineering conventional voting system through ICT: University's perspectives. Gading Business and Management Journal, 13(2), 61-76. https://ir.uitm.edu.my/id/eprint/36864/

1.2 Problem statement

Conventional ballot-paper elections take considerable time and money to run, and they often produce blank votes. Handling the process manually at both the back end and the front end also makes it hard to make sure that each student votes only once while keeping every vote secret. A web-based voting system can address these problems.

1.3 Purpose and overall idea

The Online MPP Voting System lets eligible students log in, vote for candidates and view the results through a web browser. The system verifies every voter, accepts one vote per voter, stores votes without linking them to the voter's identity, counts the votes automatically when voting closes, and publishes results only after an administrator has verified them.

The purpose of the system is to make student elections faster, fairer and more accessible.The overall idea is simple: a student proves who they are, casts one secret vote, and the system counts and publishes the results without manual handling.


1.4 Context of use

The system is used during an MPP election period set by the election committee. Students access it from a web browser on a computer or phone. Before voting opens, the administrator enters the candidates. While voting is open, voters log in and vote. After voting closes, the system counts the votes and the administrator verifies the results before they are shown to everyone.
